# Bundled Apache RAT policy references

The RAT Policy Manager includes MD-Editor-authored templates and limited validation schemas derived from publicly documented Apache RAT configuration concepts. The validation schemas are intentionally identified as MD-Editor subsets; they are not generated or official Apache RAT schemas.
